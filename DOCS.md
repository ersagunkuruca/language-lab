# Docs

Every document in the repository, by what you'd read it for. The status
says how much of what a doc describes exists today: **current** (describes
what's built), **built** (a design note whose steps are all done),
**partly built** (some steps done, the rest are backlog rows), **proposal**
(nothing built yet), or **history** (kept as written, superseded).
Statuses were checked on 2026-10-30. When a step of a design note is done,
update its line here in the same PR.

Dated documents (`REVIEW-*`, `QA-*`, `*-2026-*`) are reports of one day.
They are never updated, and their findings are rows in
[BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md).
