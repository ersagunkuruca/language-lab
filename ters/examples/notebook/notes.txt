# a few notes, then questions about them
note harbour The tide comes in twice a day, and the boats in the harbour rise with it.
note lighthouse A lighthouse keeps ships off the rocks; its light turns all night, tide or no tide.
note moon The moon pulls the sea, which makes the tides. A full moon makes the highest tides.
note bakery The bakery on the harbour opens at five. Its bread is gone by nine.
note ferries Ferries cross the bay every hour, but not at low tide, when the bay is mud.
note owls Owls hunt at night by sound; their faces gather it like dishes.
