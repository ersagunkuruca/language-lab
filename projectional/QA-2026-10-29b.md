# Editor click-through QA, 2026-10-29 (second pass)

The second pass today (QA10). It follows [QA-2026-10-29.md](QA-2026-10-29.md) (QA9) and looks at the batch since then: the embeddable editor (#620 to #624: `?embed`, `&readonly`, `&code=` and Copy embed link, the tutorial's embeds), Tidy everything and auto-folding (#613, #614, #616, #619, #625), folded box widths (#626), clipped texts and matches (#627, #628), `(…) =>` frame titles (#629), the selected item drawn on top (#631), and the Tumble, Lockstep and nested to-do examples.

**Build tested:** `main` at `0fb9580` (#631), then again at `2080d3f` (#633) for the graph checks, since #632 and #633 landed during the pass. Built with `npm run build-editor`, served over `http://localhost` (so the clipboard and the tutorial's iframes behave as on the live page) and driven in headless Chromium with playwright-core. Screenshots are in [qa/2026-10-29b/](qa/2026-10-29b/).

## What works

- **Every example opens and every `main` runs.** 19 programs, 247 definitions. Each text `main` printed what it should (Numbers & physics, Insertion sort, Eight queens' 92, Countdown's expressions, Calculus, Run backwards, Conjugation, Relations, Effects, Lockstep's position and velocity). The page programs (dice, Pig, Tumble, to-do, converter, pedal) draw their pages. No page error and no console error, apart from the missing favicon (L5) and Chromium's expected WebGPU warning.
- **The tutorial's two embeds** (`insert`, and `sort` read-only) show the definition's blocks over Run, inside the 560 px frame (`tutorial-embed-insert.png`, `tutorial-embed-sort-readonly.png`). At 380 px wide the frame scrolls inside itself and its Run inputs stay reachable (`tutorial-embed-phone.png`, `embed-phone-scrolled.png`).
- **An embed's Run is live.** `x = 7` gives `[1, 3, 7]`. Ticking result and unticking items runs it backwards (`embed-backwards.png`). Clicking a part shows its type and what can replace it. Delete leaves a typed hole, as in the editor.
- **`&readonly` holds.** Delete, typing and right-click change nothing, and the status says "This snippet is read-only: Open in the editor to change it." Run's inputs still change (`embed-readonly.png`).
- **Copy embed link** (Program tab) copies `?embed&program=sort&def=insert` for an untouched example. After an edit it copies a 3.4 KB `&code=` link instead. That link shows the edited `insert`, hole included (`embed-code.png`). Its Open in the editor adds it to the menu as "Snippet": "Opened the snippet from the link as a program of your own."
- **Folded boxes stay narrow.** 128 folded boxes over the 247 definitions measure 77 to 202 px wide, with a median of 154.
- **Frame titles** read `(…) =>` or `(…) with rest =>` past 24 characters. Every other title is at most 24 (`(rest, moment, board) =>`, `(w, rest, rest shown) =>`). No graph text is longer than 36 characters, apart from definition headers and type names.
- **No node overlaps another**, on opening or after Tidy everything, in any of the 247 definitions.
- **Tidy everything is one layout** at `2080d3f`: a second Tidy says "Already tidy" everywhere. At `0fb9580` it didn't. In six definitions each Tidy moved the same nodes again by 1 px (Lockstep `step` 28 nodes, Tumble `show next` 102). #633 fixed that during the pass.
- **Fold and unfold come back to the same place** at `2080d3f` (#632). Each of Pig `main`'s four folded handlers unfolds and refolds at the same spot. Ersagun's report about this, and about Tidy inside Pig's "play a game" handler, are being handled by the Editor lane (#632, #633), so they aren't listed again below.
- **The nested to-do outline** works in the Run pane. Tick marks an item, "add a step" puts a step under its item, one level in. A typed item followed by Enter lands at the end. The count follows ("2 of 9 done", `todo-after.png`).
- **QA9's findings are all done:** I30 (#599), I31 (#609), I32 (#610), I33 (#605), I34 (#582). No Run panel shows a hash or `NaN`.

## How to repeat

As in [QA-2026-10-29.md](QA-2026-10-29.md). New this time:

- **Serve, don't open from disk:** `python3 -m http.server` at the repo root, so the tutorial's iframes and the clipboard work. Give the context `permissions: ["clipboard-read", "clipboard-write"]` to read what Copy embed link copied.
- **Embeds:** `index.html?embed&program=sort&def=insert`, `&readonly`, and the `&code=` link from Copy embed link after an edit. The embed's Run inputs are `main.embed input[type=text]`, and a solved port is an `output`.
- **Slow opens:** the time a click on `[data-def]` takes to return is a fair measure: it waits for the page's thread. A `requestAnimationFrame` gap recorder missed H1's 9 s. A `PerformanceObserver` on `longtask` caught it.
- **Tidy:** on each freshly opened graph, Tidy everything should say "Already tidy" at once, and again the second time.
- **Overlaps:** compare the `getBBox()` × `getCTM()` boxes of every `rect.cn` and `rect.rg.folded`.
