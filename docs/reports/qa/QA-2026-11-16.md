## What works

- **All 35 programs open and run,** with every definition opened. Every text `main` gives its expected output. The console showed only Chromium's WebGPU warning, which is expected without a GPU. The 404 is gone (I306).
- **A reload opens the definition you were on.** After Pig → `turn` and a reload, `turn` is open again (I302).
