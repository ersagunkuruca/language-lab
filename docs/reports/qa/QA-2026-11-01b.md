## What works

- **Every example's `main`.** All 22 programs were opened, every definition of each. Every text `main` printed what it should: physics, sort, queens, Countdown, calculus, Run backwards, conjugation, relations, effects and Lockstep. No page or console error came up except F2 below (and Chromium's expected WebGPU warning).
- **QA12's H1 is fixed.** No Clifford helper freezes on opening now. The slowest is bounce's `geometric product` at 0.9 to 1.3 s (QA12 had 2.4 to 3.7 s for several), and Constructions' `equal arcs` took 0.9 s on the first walk. QA12's M1 helpers (dice's `landed`, the to-do's `field`) now open without the failed search.
- **Resize corner (#914).** It sits over the selected node, is easy to grab (18 px), and dragging it grows the node with its wires following. Dragging it back past the node's own size stops at that size.
- **Pinch and drag with fingers (#917),** tried with touch events. A two-finger pinch zooms (100% → 300%). A node dragged with one finger follows it, and a second finger hands over to the pinch without the node jumping. A long press opens the menu.
- **Spreading the picked nodes (#918).** Two picked nodes get a dashed frame with four corners. Dragging a corner spreads them, and Undo puts them back.
- **List sockets (#922)** show in all four page mains tried (dice, to-do, Pig, converter: 6 to 13 lists each), as a bracket with a socket per item (`dice-sockets.png`). The + socket adds a hole at the end of its list (about 1.2 s), and the Undo button takes it out.
- **Turning a call around (#923, #928).** In `root`, the menu's "Work out a from the result" turns `discriminant(a, b, c)` into `solve discriminant(result = a, …) for a`. A double-click on its first input port does the same, and Undo puts it back. Calls name their ports (#927).
- **Make it a function and Make it a definition** on the two `·` nodes in `fall` give `part`, with `drift, drop` as named results at `c0b6c52` (#948): `(speed, time, in3, in4) → drift, drop => speed · time, in3 · in4`. Run still gives `holds = true`. The result wire is bundled, labelled with its parts, and fans out into two strands when its label is clicked. Undo restores the text exactly. "Make it a definition" on the folded `part` function moves it to the side menu.

## How to repeat

As in [QA-2026-11-01.md](QA-2026-11-01.md). New this time:

- **Menu timing:** right-click a node's centre and time how long until `.ctx-menu` shows. Then poll the menu's `[data-menu]` buttons every 100 ms until nothing more turns `disabled`; that's when its checks are done. The checks run one per `setTimeout` after it opens (`checkLater` in editor/main.ts).
- **Why a menu is slow:** build with `minify: false, keepNames: true` on line 31 of editor/build.ts (don't commit it), then run a CDP `Profiler` round one right-click and add up the time by function.
- **Touch:** `newContext({ hasTouch: true })`, then CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` with two touch points for a pinch. The zoom is the `%` in `.p-graph`'s text.
- **Sockets:** `.p-graph rect.list-port`, and the + sockets are `.p-graph [data-add]`. Bundled wires' labels are `.bundle-l`, clicked through `[data-fan]`. A fanned wire's strands are `path.strand`.
- **Wire errors:** after opening each definition, look in `.side`'s text for "No values on the wires:" followed by something other than a reason in plain words.
