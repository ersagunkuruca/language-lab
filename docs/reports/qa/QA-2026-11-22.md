## What works

- **All 50 programs open, with every definition, and run.** Every text `main` gives its expected output. The console showed only Chromium's "No available adapters" warning, which is expected without a GPU. There's no overflow flood after the full walk (I333).
- **Programs load quickly now that they're read on opening.** Most definition lists showed within 0.1 s. The slowest were Bigger program: moths (1.9 s), break (1.2 s) and board (1.1 s).
