## What works

- **`newton`'s type reads without `e`.** The blocks show `newton : ((Float64) -[Choice, Diverge, …]-> Float64, (Float64) -[Choice, Diverge, …]-> Float64, Float64) -[Choice, Diverge, …]-> Float64`. Hovering it says "… is whatever f and slope do when called: the effects of the functions it's given". No `e]->` is left in calculus's blocks.
- **`queens` has one frame**, `(row, rows, board) =>`: `nil`, then `cons(column, board)` and else `fail()` go straight into their ports. `magnitude` has no frame at all, and its else is one wire from `x`.
- **`rotate` has one `for some` node and one `(length, angle) =>` frame.** After Tidy, nothing overlaps.
- **A dot put on a wire** in `magnitude` sits on the output of the `0` it shares. The Changes tab then lists `magnitude` as changed, marks 3 parts in the graph, and its diff reads `+ x₂ = 0`, `~ if x < x₂ then −x else x`. Undo says "Undid: the code, the names and the layout changed back."
- **The Guide** has its "Text you can type" heading.
- **Opening every physics definition** takes under 0.6 s each.
- **No page errors** and no console errors.
