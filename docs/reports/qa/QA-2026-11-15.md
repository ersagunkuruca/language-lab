# Editor click-through QA, 2026-11-15

This pass follows today's editor changes:

- **E265 (#2750):** the graph can number the calls with effects in the order they run. The canvas menu turns it on and off, and the setting is kept in layout.json.
- **I296 (#2751):** a type held while a hole is open holds the type only, not a pin's units.
- **L252e (#2747):** a pin keeps the units its definition's numbers carry, and the palette refuses a fill that changes them.

It follows [QA-2026-11-14b.md](QA-2026-11-14b.md).

**Build tested:** `main` at `a133f29` (#2753), built with `node editor/build.ts` and opened from the file in headless Chromium (Playwright). There's no GPU here. The full browser smoke wasn't run, following Ersagun's note of 2026-10-29.

## Findings

| # | Finding | Repro | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **A pin's units show in the text only.** Once `rate` is pinned, the text says `(Float64 u, Float64 v) -[Choice]-> Float64 u/v`, but three other places say plain `(Float64, Float64) -[Choice]-> Float64`: the pin row ("Pinned at …"), the status note after pinning, and the graph's signature line. The hole's "needs Float64" also leaves out the `u` the palette then holds it to. So the reason a fill is refused ("pinned to … u/v") names units that nothing else on screen shows ([pin-dt.png](screenshots/2026-11-15/pin-dt.png)). | New program → `rate(d, t) = d / t` → select `rate` → Pin this type. Compare the text, the graph's title and the pin row. | Medium |
| Q2 | **A folded frame hides the numbers inside it.** In crew's `main`, the `[path]` arm is folded, so the program's real work (read file, plan of, report) has no visible number. Only the two `usage` calls are numbered, both 1. A folded frame holding numbered calls could show their range on its header (say "1–4") ([crew-order.png](screenshots/2026-11-15/crew-order.png)). | crew → `main` → canvas menu → Number the effects. | Low |
| Q3 | **Two numberings side by side.** The purple sequencing dots carry numbers too. In clinic, the dot after `write file` is 1 and its call is 4 ([clinic-order.png](screenshots/2026-11-15/clinic-order.png)). In Numbers & physics, dots 1–7 sit next to calls 1–7, where they happen to agree ([numbers-order.png](screenshots/2026-11-15/numbers-order.png)). With the overlay on, a reader can mix the two up. One option is to hide the dots' numbers while the overlay is on. | clinic → `main` → Number the effects. | Low |
| Q4 | **A number can sit on text.** Effects' badge 1 covers the end of the signature line (`Unit`). crew's inner `usage` badge covers the `other` in its frame's header `(path, other, more) =>` ([effects-order.png](screenshots/2026-11-15/effects-order.png), [crew-order.png](screenshots/2026-11-15/crew-order.png)). | As above. | Low |
| Q5 | **A reload opens the program on `main`, not on the definition you were editing.** After editing `rate` in a new program and reloading, the editor shows `main` with its hole selected ([reload.png](screenshots/2026-11-15/reload.png)). The program and the held types are kept. This is what I235 does (the start definition's first hole), but it loses your place. | New program → define and edit `rate` → reload. | Low |

2. Q2 and Q4: the overlay's placement (a folded frame's range, and badges placed clear of text).
3. Q3.
4. Q5, if keeping your place is wanted. It's a choice about I235's behaviour.

## How to repeat

As in [QA-2026-11-09.md](QA-2026-11-09.md). Wait for `[data-set=program]` before clicking. To reach the canvas menu, right-click an empty point of `.p-graph svg.canvas` (one where `document.elementFromPoint` is the svg itself), then click `.ctx-item[data-menu="order"]`. The numbers are `.p-graph .effn`. To pin, click the definition's name (`.blocks-wrap .x.def`), then the `.pin-row` button.
