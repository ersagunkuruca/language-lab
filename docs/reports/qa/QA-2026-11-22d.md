## What works

- **Moving between definitions** takes about 0.1 s in every program, and no switch made a long task over 0.21 s.
- **Run** on the command-line programs finishes in under a quarter of a second.
- **Tidy** on each main takes under 0.2 s. Every main was already tidy.
- **No page errors** came up in the whole pass.
