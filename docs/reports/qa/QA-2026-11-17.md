## What works

- **All 35 programs open and run,** with every definition opened, and every text `main` gives its expected output. The console showed only Chromium's WebGPU warning.
- **Scrubbing a number works.** I selected `0.5` in `fall`'s `drop`, dragged 50 px right on its field in the Selected tab, and it read `0.545` while dragging. Run solved again as it changed, and the status line said "Changed a literal." (`fall-scrub.png`). One Undo gave `0.5` back. Pressing Esc in the middle of a drag also put `0.5` back, with nothing to undo.
- **The new wording reads well.** The Selected tab's buttons are now ↑ Outer, Copy, Replace with…, Wrap in…, Clear, Add before, Add after, Earlier, Later and Remove. Effects' `area` says "Needs a host for Ask · or call it in `handle Ask`" in place of a failed run (`effects-area.png`).
- **Effects' `main` gives one shader reason now:** "no: main has effects (Console)" (I309).
- **Countdown's `symbol`** no longer shows up as slow (I310).
