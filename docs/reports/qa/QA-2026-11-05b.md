## What works

- **All 32 programs open,** each in 3.5 to 9 s from a fresh page, with every definition clicked. No page errors.
- **Every text `main` gives its expected output.** That covers numbers, sort, queens, countdown, calculus, run backwards, conjugate, relations, effects, lockstep, sheet (with its deliberate `#cycle`, `#div/0` and `#parse` cells), machine (its five assembler programs again: I171 holds), notebook, routes, ledger, chapters, calc, clinic and crew.
- **calc reads its Standard input box:** `1 + 2 * 3` and `(4 - 1) / 2` give 7 and 1, as `lab run` gives them.
- **Every page but the pedal runs** in the preview: outline, converter, constructions, bounce, spacetime, tumble, dice, pig and board, with clicks on their buttons.
- **E254 works end to end.** Opening a file with an older module says so, Upgrade moves `twice` from version 1 to 2 with its tests passing, the button goes away, and Run main gives 42.
- **Pure works.** Ticking Pure on effects' `Ask` says what it means, and the box stays ticked.
- **Editing gestures work:**
  - Tab steps through parts, Alt+↑ goes to the enclosing part, and Shift+F10 opens the menu.
  - Copy, cut, undo and redo all work.
  - Make it a definition works, and Undo takes it back.
  - Save file, then Open file, gives the program back as one of your own.
  - New program, then `print` and `"hi"` from the palette, then Run main, prints `hi`. An empty program's Run says "the program reached a hole".
- **Views switch quickly:** names, notation, Blocks, Steps, Nested and Lisp, and every side tab. Each takes about 0.7 s, and the browser's own wait is most of that.
- **Find works:** typing `queens` in Find and pressing Enter opens it. The embed (`?embed&program=sort&def=insert`) shows and runs.
- **The other pages open with no errors:** learn, guide, tutorial, reactive and shaders.
- **+ New definition** runs in tasks of at most 0.12 s (I167).
