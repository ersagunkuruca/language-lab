## What works

- **Every example runs.** All 30 programs were opened, with every definition, including the eight bigger programs. Every text `main` gave its expected output:
  - the sheet's budget, with its deliberate `#cycle`, `#div/0` and `#parse` cells;
  - the machine's five assembler programs, in 15 s;
  - the clinic's 137 patients;
  - notebook, routes, ledger and chapters.

  No page or console error came up, apart from Chromium's "Failed to create WebGPU Context Provider" warning, which is expected without a GPU.
- **Typing in the palette is smooth.** Five keys of `x * 2` kept every frame under 20 ms, in Numbers & physics, Insertion sort and Tumble. QA14 measured 60 to 180 ms a key (I135).
