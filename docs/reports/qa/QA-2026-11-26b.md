## What works

- **All 45 programs open, with all 1,938 of their definitions drawn, and every text `main` runs.** No page errors. The console showed only Chromium's "No available adapters" warning. The slowest definition took 1.4 s to draw (break's `moving`) while other pages were open. Alone, every one took under 0.65 s.
- **A comment is the node's top row, inside its box** (`comment-row.png`):
  - Written in the inspector's Comment box, the row appears in the box, and the box grows upward by the row.
  - No line floats above the node.
  - Hovering shows the whole comment, then the node's type.
  - The row moves with the node when dragged, and is kept after a reload.
  - Undo takes the comment away, the inspector's box follows, and Redo brings it back.
  - Tidy everything counts the grown box: after it, no two nodes overlap.
  - A folded frame shows a speech mark for the comments it hides.
  - In Blocks, the comment is a `--` line above its part.
- **Digits learns as it opens (I371):** "pass 1, batch 1 of 12" and its digits show without pressing Learn (`digits.png`).
- **machine's first Run is quicker (I372):** 3.3 s on a fresh page, against 5.7 s this morning. Eight queens took 0.65 s and calc 2.1 s.

## How to repeat

As in [QA-2026-11-25.md](QA-2026-11-25.md). For comments:

1. Select a node by clicking its `rect[data-id]` in `.p-graph`.
2. Fill `#f-comment`, then press Tab, which saves it.
3. Read the node's group: it should hold a `.cmt-row` inside the rect's bounds, and nothing should match `.cmt-line`.
